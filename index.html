Traditional Taekwondo

General Information

Discover the traditions, interesting facts, techniques and values of Taekwondo.

So, what is traditional taekwondo?

Taekwondo is a Korean martial art which developed more around the 1940's- 1950's however attains ancient roots. It is a sport that combines physical techniques, mental discipline and personal development. The name comes from three Korean words: "Tae", meaning foot or kicking, "Kwon", meaning fist or striking, and "Do", meaning the way or path of life.


It is practised around the world and includes techniques such as kicks, punches, blocks and patterns of movement. Taekwondo also teaches important values such as respect, perseverance, confidence and self-control.


Traditional Taekwondo focuses on the original principles and values of the martial art, placing importance on self-defence, discipline and the development of both the body and the mind. Rather than focusing only on physical strength, it encourages students to improve their character and apply these values in everyday life.

Interesting Facts

Did you know that Taekwondo is much more than just kicking and punching? Originating in Korea, this fascinating martial art became an official Olympic sport in 2000. It is famous for its incredibly fast kicks, impressive jumping techniques and powerful spinning movements. But there is more to Taekwondo than meets the eye. Every practitioner wears a uniform called a dobok, and their belt represents their progress and experience. Most importantly, Taekwondo teaches valuable life lessons, including respect, perseverance and self-control, proving that true strength comes from both the body and the mind.
