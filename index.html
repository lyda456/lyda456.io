Traditional Taekwondo

General Information

Discover the traditions, interesting facts, techniques and values of Taekwondo.

So, what is traditional Taekwondo?

Taekwondo is a Korean martial art that developed mainly during the 1940s and 1950s, although it draws on older Korean martial traditions. It combines physical techniques, mental discipline and personal development. The name comes from three Korean words: "Tae", meaning foot or kicking, "Kwon", meaning fist or striking, and "Do", meaning the way or path of life.

It is practised around the world and includes techniques such as kicks, punches, blocks and patterns of movement. Taekwondo also teaches important values such as respect, perseverance, confidence and self-control.

Traditional Taekwondo focuses on the principles and values of the martial art, placing importance on self-defence, discipline and the development of both the body and the mind. Rather than focusing only on physical strength, it encourages students to improve their character and apply these values in everyday life.

Interesting Facts

Did you know that Taekwondo is much more than just kicking and punching? Originating in Korea, this martial art became an official Olympic sport in 2000. It is famous for its fast kicks, jumping techniques and powerful spinning movements.

Every practitioner wears a uniform called a dobok, and their belt represents their progress and experience. Most importantly, Taekwondo teaches valuable life lessons, including respect, perseverance and self-control, proving that strength comes from both the body and the mind.

The Five Tenets

The five tenets guide Taekwondo practitioners both during training and in everyday life.

Courtesy

Showing respect towards others and treating people with kindness.

Integrity

Being honest, taking responsibility and doing what is right.

Perseverance

Continuing to work towards your goals, even when something is difficult.

Self-control

Managing your actions and emotions and thinking before you act.

Indomitable Spirit

Remaining determined and facing challenges with courage and confidence.

More About Taekwondo

The Taekwondo Uniform

The traditional Taekwondo uniform is called a dobok. It allows practitioners to move freely while training and represents discipline and respect for the martial art.

What Does Training Involve?

Taekwondo training can include warm-ups, kicking techniques, blocks, patterns, sparring and self-defence practice. Training also encourages focus, coordination and perseverance.
